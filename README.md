
I'm participating in an AI x Fintech hackathon in NYC on October 17, where I plan to build an AI-powered personal finance decision-making agent.

The idea is to help individuals navigate complex financial trade-offs by analyzing their cash flow, outstanding debts, and long-term financial goals.

For example, if someone has $900 in monthly disposable income, should they prioritize paying off high-interest credit card debt, accelerating student loan repayments, or saving toward a down payment on a future home?

The agent would simulate different allocation strategies, quantify their impact on financial goals, and explain the trade-offs so users can make more informed decisions.
